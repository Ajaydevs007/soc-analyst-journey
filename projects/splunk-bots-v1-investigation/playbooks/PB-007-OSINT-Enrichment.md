# Playbook: Threat Intelligence Enrichment

## Playbook ID

PB-007

---

# Objective

Enrich Indicators of Compromise (IOCs) using trusted OSINT sources.

---

# Procedure

1. Identify suspicious domain or IP.
2. Query WHOIS records.
3. Review historical passive DNS.
4. Search VirusTotal and SecurityTrails.
5. Review threat intelligence reports.
6. Document findings and confidence level.
7. Record any investigation limitations.

---

# Analyst Notes

Historical attribution should always be distinguished from current, independently verifiable evidence.
