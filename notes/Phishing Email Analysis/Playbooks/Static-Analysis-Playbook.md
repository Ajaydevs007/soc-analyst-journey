# Static Analysis Playbook

## Objective

Collect evidence without executing suspicious content.

---

## Step 1

Do NOT click anything.

---

## Step 2

Inspect URLs.

Collect:

- Domain
- Subdomain
- Path
- Parameters

---

## Step 3

Check Domain.

Tools:

- WHOIS
- VirusTotal
- URLScan
- MXToolbox

Collect:

- Age
- Registrar
- Reputation

---

## Step 4

Check IP Reputation.

Tools:

- Cisco Talos
- AbuseIPDB
- VirusTotal

Collect:

- Reputation
- Blacklists
- ASN

---

## Step 5

Inspect Attachments.

Collect:

- File Name
- Extension
- File Size
- Hash

---

## Step 6

Search Hash.

Tools:

- VirusTotal
- Hybrid Analysis

---

## Step 7

Inspect Email Content.

Look for:

- Urgency
- Threats
- Credential Requests
- Payment Requests
- Grammar Errors

---

## Evidence Collected

- URLs
- Domains
- Hashes
- IPs
- Reputation
- Attachments
