# Introduction to Phishing

## What is Phishing?

A phishing attack is a social engineering attack that attempts to trick users into revealing sensitive information or executing malicious content.

Common goals of phishing attacks include:

- Stealing usernames and passwords
- Delivering malware
- Gaining initial access to an organization
- Financial fraud
- Credential harvesting

Phishing attacks generally occur through:

- Emails
- SMS messages (Smishing)
- Voice calls (Vishing)
- Fake websites

---

## Phishing in the Cyber Kill Chain

Phishing belongs to the **Delivery** phase of the Cyber Kill Chain.

### Attack Flow

↓

↓

↓

Actions on Objectives

The attacker delivers malicious content to the victim through email or another communication channel.

---

## Common Social Engineering Techniques

Attackers often use psychological triggers such as:

- "You have won a gift."
- "Your account will be suspended."
- "Urgent action required."
- "Verify your account immediately."
- "Limited time offer."

These techniques exploit the human factor, which is often considered the weakest security layer.

---

## Purpose of Phishing

The main objective of phishing is usually not the email itself but obtaining initial access into the target environment.

Phishing may lead to:

- Credential theft
- Malware execution
- Ransomware deployment
- Business Email Compromise (BEC)
- Internal network compromise

---

## Key Takeaway

Phishing attacks exploit human trust and are commonly used as the first step of a cyber attack.
